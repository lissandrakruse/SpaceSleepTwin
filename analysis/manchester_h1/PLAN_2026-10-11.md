# Manchester H1 analysis plan — recorded before first model run
Date: 2026-10-11 (America/Sao_Paulo). Source tables have already been inspected for schema, missingness and units; this is a prospective record of this analysis, not an untouched-cohort preregistration.

## Question and interpretation
Does measured pre-sleep melanopic exposure add predictive information about the published diary hoursToSleep field beyond strictly prior personal latency history? Primary comparison adds both log1p(presleep3hr) and TAT250 to the historical reference. Separate pre-sleep and daytime ablations are reported, without selecting a winner as confirmatory evidence.

This is retrospective exposure-conditioned prediction with later-date evaluation. The source presleep3hr window ends at observed Fitbit startTime, so it is NOT a deployable before-bedtime predictor or a causal test. A future operational model requires a separately reconstructed window ending at previously known bedtime.

## Locked inputs and quality gates
Upstream commit ef570332cd6bd04cafea40a66f5a70e6f2206505; use light_daily_data.RData and sleep_diary.RData with SHA-256 checked against existing import audit. Join Id,yday4 one-to-one. Keep hoursToSleep >=0 and finite; convert hoursToSleep*60 to minutes. Use that published field, not datetimeSleep-datetimeGobed; observed disagreements between latency field and derived clock differences must be counted and disclosed. Source preprocessing and datetime units need clarification before biological validation.
presleep3hr is a zero-inflated log/exponential summary from measured MEDI, not arithmetic-mean lux. The chosen additional log1p transform is an explicitly specified modeling transform, not reconstruction of the upstream function. TAT250 is minutes above 250 melanopic lux. Exclude missing/nonfinite/negative exposure from all paired comparisons. No outlier removal or participant-level record redistribution while exact reuse license is unresolved.
Use datetimeGobed as UTC converted to Europe/London and assign each study night by local datetime minus four hours. Verify uniqueness of participant/night; fail on duplicates.

## Histories and models
For each target night require >=3 strictly earlier diary nights from the same person. Previous nights may lack matched light, since their diary latency is known. History = median latency over all prior diary nights. No future outcome in any history, no personal z-score.
Models: personal median; personal median + training residual median; personal median + median residual regression on pre-sleep light alone; daytime TAT250 alone; both exposures. QuantileRegressor(quantile=0.5,alpha=0.05,solver=highs) with training-only StandardScaler. Fixed alpha, no tuning. Light residual regressions include an intercept; the residual-median baseline isolates intercept adjustment.
Secondary sensitivity: joint-exposure quantile model in log1p latency residual scale, transformed back with expm1. No clipping of negative raw model outputs; report their counts. Do not replace primary results with the sensitivity winner.

## Temporal evaluation and metrics
Partition eligible unique dates into four consecutive equal-count date blocks. First initializes training; evaluate remaining blocks using strictly earlier dates. No same-date training/test overlap. Fail rather than silently change protocol if a training fold has <20 nights or <5 people. History can update with strictly earlier evaluated outcomes, representing updated known-person observations.
Primary: night-weighted MAE in minutes. Also RMSE, median absolute error, participant-macro MAE, and negative prediction counts. All models must share identical test keys. Bootstrap 2000 participant clusters of fixed paired errors, seed 20261011; compare joint-exposure primary model with personal-history and residual-median references. Report percentile intervals; ignore neither conditional nature nor cross-person calendar dependence. No causal inference, significance claim or biological MDE calculation.
Describe diary reported-minute frequency and proportions at multiples of 5/10/15, without claiming a hard minimum possible error.

## Persistence and completion
Publish this plan before running models. Publish reproducible code, aggregate audit and aggregate results only; preserve private record-level predictions outside public repo. State all exclusions and source discrepancies. These results do not validate Forger99, DLMO or astronauts.
